Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services, process, and pricing.

How do I get started?

Choose a service package that fits your needs, click "Choose" to open our inquiry form, and tell us about your project. We'll get back to you within 24 hours with a quote and timeline.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal and Stripe (credit/debit cards). Payment is due before work begins. For larger projects, we offer milestone-based payments.

How many revisions are included?

Most packages include 1-2 revision rounds. Additional revisions can be requested at $15-40 per round depending on complexity.

What file formats do you deliver?

Video: MP4 (H.264). Audio: WAV, MP3, FLAC. Images: PNG, JPG, WebP. 3D: PLY, GLB, OBJ (scoped per engine). We can accommodate other formats on request.

Can I use the deliverables commercially?

Yes. All custom deliverables are licensed for commercial use. Royalty-free assets from our shop are cleared for use per package terms. Custom LoRAs and trained models are yours to keep.

What if my video is longer than the included duration?

Videos exceeding the covered duration are quoted on brief based on actual length and complexity. Per-minute rates apply beyond the included duration (see our Duration & Pricing Guide).

Do you offer rush/priority processing?

Yes, rush processing is available for an additional 25-50% fee depending on urgency and current queue. Contact us for a rush quote.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. All processing is done locally on our hardware. Your media files are never uploaded to cloud services. We can sign NDAs for sensitive projects.
